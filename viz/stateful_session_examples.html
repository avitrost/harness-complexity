Concrete examples of session, replay, and recovery mechanics

Real gpt-5.4-mini low TB2 trace snippets showing what I mean by persistent sessions, structured replay, multi-call turns, and post-failure continuation. The point is to show both examples and counterexamples, not to claim any one flag is causal.

Selected-turn CSV: /wbl-fast/usrs/trost/harness-complexity/final_test/behavior_gpt54_low_family_20260612/attribute_driver_analysis/stateful_examples_turns.csv

Definitions

Session A turn with metadata.unified_exec.session_id. This is process/shell continuity, not merely file persistence. Files under /app persist even without a session.
Structured replay A turn with codex_response_items or mini_swe_agent_v2_messages. The harness keeps assistant/tool-call history as structured items instead of only flattened text.
Multi-call A Codex turn where codex_emitted_tool_calls > 1. One model response emitted a small sequence of tool calls.

A useful recoverable loop is usually a combination: failed command outcome, structured observation, continued work, and sometimes session/process continuity. The counterexamples below are why I do not reduce it to raw turns or raw session count.
